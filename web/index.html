    How it works

    Houses ~30 m apart. Radio reaches ~100 m.

    Every phone hears three houses each way. The street is already a network.

    1. 1

      Discover

      Over BLE. No pairing, no server.

    2. 2

      Relay

      Queue, then forward on contact. Up to 8 hops.

    3. 3

      Log

      A GPS breadcrumb every 60 s, offline.

    4. 4

      Harvest

      Sync once, pull the cluster’s heatmap.

    Who it serves

    One network, two jobs.

    For victimsBe found.

    • Reach family with zero signal
    • One-tap panic beacon
    • Request exactly what you need
    • Coordinate inside a cut-off cluster

    For respondersWalk in with a map.

    • Harvest heatmaps from any node
    • Triage by priority tag
    • See hazards before committing a team
    • Route by density, not door to door
